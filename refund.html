Customer Protection

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Fair, transparent, and prompt refund processing for all customers of Savera Paradise.

1. Overview

At Savera Paradise (owned and operated by ABDUL PEER SAB), customer satisfaction is our highest priority. Because we prepare perishable cooked food items fresh to order, our cancellation and refund policies are designed to be clear, fair, and in full compliance with Indian Consumer Protection regulations and Cashfree Payments aggregator guidelines.

2. Order Cancellation Policy

Due to the perishable and custom-prepared nature of cooked meals, the following cancellation guidelines apply:

  • Immediate Cancellation Window: You may cancel your order free of charge within one (1) minute of placement before the restaurant kitchen confirms and begins cooking your order.
  • Once Food Preparation Has Begun: Once our chefs begin preparation (order status transitions to Preparing), orders cannot be cancelled or altered because ingredients have already been dedicated and cooked.
  • Cancellation by Savera Paradise: In the rare event that an item is out of stock or our kitchen is unable to fulfill your order due to unforeseen circumstances, we will cancel your order immediately and issue a 100% full refund.

3. Refund Eligibility Criteria

You are eligible for a replacement or a complete refund in any of the following scenarios:

  1. Damaged, Spilled or Tampered Food: If the packaging was compromised, spilled, or damaged during transit.
  2. Wrong Items Delivered: If the items delivered do not match the dishes you ordered.
  3. Missing Items: If any paid item was missing from your delivery package (partial refund for the missing item).
  4. Non-Delivery: If our delivery rider is unable to deliver your order due to a restaurant or courier logistical failure.
  5. Payment Deduction Without Order Confirmation: If money was debited from your bank account or UPI app via Cashfree, but the order failed to generate on our system.

4. Refund Timeframe & Mode of Settlement

Mandatory 5–7 Working Days Settlement:
Once your refund request is verified and approved by our support team, the refund is initiated immediately through our authorized payment partner, Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd.

Refunds are credited back directly to the original source of payment (e.g. Bank Account via UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, or Net Banking) within 5 to 7 business days, subject to your issuing bank's processing cycles.

5. Cash on Delivery (COD) Refunds

If you paid via Cash on Delivery and are eligible for a refund due to a verified issue, our manager will initiate a direct UPI transfer (via PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm) to your verified mobile number or bank account within 24 hours.
