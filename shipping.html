Shipping & Delivery Policy

Our commitment to delivering hot, hygienic, and fresh meals swiftly to your doorstep.

1. Scope & Service Coverage Area

Savera Paradise (owned and operated by ABDUL PEER SAB) offers direct doorstep cooked food delivery across Ramanagara City and neighboring residential areas within an operational road radius of up to 10 kilometers from our central kitchen at Main Road, Ramanagara, Karnataka.

Customers located outside our designated operational boundary will be notified at checkout that delivery is currently unserviceable to their pinned address.

2. Delivery Timeframes

Typical Delivery Timeline: 30 to 45 Minutes
From the moment you complete checkout and payment via Cashfree Payments or choose COD:
  • Kitchen Cooking & Packing: 12 to 18 minutes (fresh preparation).
  • Transit & Road Travel: 15 to 25 minutes depending on distance and local traffic.

During peak dinner rush hours, adverse monsoon weather, or festive holidays, delivery times may occasionally extend up to 50–60 minutes. Our dispatch team will keep you informed via live status updates.

3. Delivery Charges & Thresholds

We maintain a completely transparent delivery pricing structure:

  • Free Home Delivery: Applied automatically on all orders with a subtotal of ₹250 or more.
  • Standard Delivery Fee: For orders below ₹250, a nominal delivery charge of ₹40 is added to support our fleet riders and fuel costs.
  • Packaging Charges: We use eco-friendly food-grade insulated tamper-evident packaging at zero extra packaging cost.

4. Live GPS Order Tracking

Once your parcel is handed over to our fleet rider at Counter 1, you can track their live location in real time on our digital tracking screen using integrated Google Maps & OSRM routing.

5. Delivery Protocol & Customer Availability

Our rider will arrive at your pinned drop location and attempt to contact you via mobile phone or WhatsApp. Please ensure your contact number is reachable upon rider arrival.

If a customer is unreachable for more than 10 minutes after arrival at the doorstep, our kitchen manager will be notified. Prepaid orders may be safely dropped at the security gate or doorstep upon your WhatsApp confirmation.
