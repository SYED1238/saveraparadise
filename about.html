About Savera Paradise

Serving flavorful, hygienic, and authentic delicacies to food lovers across Ramanagara.

1. Who We Are

Founded and managed by proprietor ABDUL PEER SAB, Savera Paradise is a premier quick-service culinary restaurant located in the heart of Ramanagara, Karnataka. We specialize in authentic Dum Biryanis, signature Crispy Fried Chicken, charcoal-grilled Tandoori and Kebabs, stone-baked artisan Pizzas, Smash Burgers, and refreshing beverages.

2. Our Culinary Promise

  • 100% Fresh Halal Ingredients: Freshly procured poultry, vegetables, and aromatic whole spices daily.
  • No Compromise on Hygiene: Inspected kitchens conforming to strict FSSAI safety norms.
  • Swift 30–45 Minute Delivery: Dedicated fleet riders delivering straight from Counter 1 to your home.
  • Seamless Digital Payments: Partnered with Cashfree Payments for safe 1-click UPI and card checkouts.
