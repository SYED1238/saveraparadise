Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026. Committed to protecting your personal information and privacy.

1. Scope & Controller Identification

This Privacy Policy explains how ABDUL PEER SAB doing business as Savera Paradise ("we", "our", or "us"), collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information obtained from customers who visit our website, place orders, or use our digital food ordering services.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with Savera Paradise, we collect only the necessary information required to fulfill your food orders:

  • Contact Information: Full name, phone number, and email address.
  • Delivery Address: House / flat number, street name, area, landmark, and GPS coordinates (with your permission) to navigate our delivery riders to your doorstep.
  • Order Details: Items purchased, total bill amount, order timestamps, and instructions for the kitchen.
  • Device & Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and cookies used to maintain your shopping cart across sessions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To process, cook, pack, and deliver your food orders.
  • To send SMS / WhatsApp notifications regarding order preparation and live rider GPS location.
  • To process refunds or customer service requests through our payment gateway.
  • To prevent fraudulent orders, abuse, or unauthorized access.

4. Payment Security & Financial Information

Strict Financial Non-Storage Policy:
We do NOT collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, debit card details, CVV codes, UPI MPINs, or net banking passwords on our servers. All electronic transactions are redirected through encrypted channels to Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd., which complies with PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) Level 1 and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) payment security directives.

5. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share necessary delivery information (customer name, delivery address, and contact number) with our assigned fleet delivery riders to enable them to bring your food to your doorstep. All our riders are verified and bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. Data Retention & Rights

We retain order history records as required for accounting, taxation, and regulatory compliance under Indian law. You have the right to request access to your stored profile data or request deletion of your account by contacting our Grievance Officer.
